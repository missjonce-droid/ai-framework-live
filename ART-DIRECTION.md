# AI Framework visual direction

The company and product name remains AI Framework. This concept uses an abstract, nonhuman presence rendered as flowing cyan/violet filaments, spacious geometric typography, and a nearly black editorial layout. It is a visual identity, not a claim that the site has a conscious agent.

Hero asset: static/images/framework-entity-v1.webp (WebP encoding of the generated original; no content changes). Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then integrated as decorative HTML imagery. Slow motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Original robot concept was not committed to the site.

Final artwork prompt:

Edit the website hero artwork. Replace the entire humanoid robot, face, head, shoulders and body with a beautiful non-anthropomorphic abstract intelligence presence occupying the right half. It is a sculptural flowing field of thousands of fine luminous filaments, bending into a distinctive asymmetric folded loop like a ribbon of energy suspended in space, with a dark hollow and delicate branching threads. Use crisp cyan, indigo and violet light with a few pale silver highlights. Sophisticated, mysterious, graceful, dimensional high-end CGI, rich depth and precise filament detail, not a generic glowing ball. Preserve the nearly black negative space in the left half for real HTML typography and the flowing fine particle field along the lower part. Full bleed wide landscape, no text, no logos, no UI, no border. No human, no face, no eyes, no robot, no body, no brain, no neural-network diagram, no globe.
