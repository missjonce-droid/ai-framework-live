College football · sportsbook market data

Market board, not a prediction

Browse current US bookmaker moneyline, spread, and totals markets gathered from The Odds API. Prices are shown with their provider timestamps. A short AI-generated summary may describe the supplied market snapshot only; it is not a statistically validated forecast.

Important limitations Implied percentages are mathematical conversions of listed prices and include bookmaker margin (vig); they are not independently calibrated chances of winning. Average lines are derived only from actual sportsbook markets in the feed. No team-statistics or injury dataset is connected, and no projected score, pick, or betting advice is provided.

Set the Worker URL in the page metadata to load market data.

Source: The Odds API v4, US region. Bookmaker offerings and freshness vary; this feed is refreshed by GitHub Actions every six hours when configured. Always verify current prices with the source.
