Privacy Policy — AI Framework

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information AI Framework ("we," "us," "our") collects when you visit ai-framework.io — including our AI tools directory, Prompt Lab, free playbooks, paid products, user accounts, and member pages — and how we use it.

Site operator: AI Framework LLC
Contact: hello@ai-framework.io

1. Information we collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Email address — when you request the free playbooks, contact us, create an account, or subscribe to emails from us. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.
  • Account information — if you sign in, we store your email address, your saved frameworks and favorites, and any member pages you publish. Sign-in uses a magic link sent to your email; we do not store a password for your account.
  • Name and message content — when you contact us.
  • Purchase and subscription information — when you buy the Playbooks Vault ($39 one-time) or subscribe to a paid plan (Pro $29/month, Business $99/month, Agency $299/month). Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card number. Stripe collects the payment details needed to complete your purchase or subscription (see "Payments" below).
  • Member pages — pages you publish and outcome notes you post are public by your choice. Outcome notes are self-reported unless clearly marked verified.

Affiliate link interactions:

  • Some outbound links on ai-framework.io are affiliate links (labeled where they appear). Clicking an affiliate link may place a cookie from the affiliate network or vendor behind the link so that we are credited if you make a purchase. Those networks collect referral data under their own privacy policies; we do not receive your payment or account details from them.

Information collected automatically:

  • Click and usage analytics matching our published disclosure: when you click a tool's outbound link (our /go/<slug> redirect), we record the tool clicked, timestamp, an anonymized referrer, browser device type, and country derived from your IP. We do not store your IP address in click history or link click data to your identity.
  • Page views, save counts, share counts, and similar aggregate counters — none linked to a user identity.
  • Basic technical data such as device type, browser type, and pages visited, collected through Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager (usage statistics) and Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps and session-replay analytics). These tools set cookies — see section 5 (Cookies).
  • Cookies or similar storage used to remember preferences such as site theme, to keep you signed in, and to store frameworks you save on your own device when you are not signed in, plus the analytics cookies described in section 5.

We do not collect sensitive personal data, and our content is not directed at children under 13 (see section 8).

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the free playbooks PDF and any bonuses you request.
  • To process Vault purchases and subscription payments, and to apply your plan's limits.
  • To operate your account, including your saved frameworks, favorites, and member pages.
  • To send emails you opted into (you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email).
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Payments

Vault purchases and subscription payments are processed by Stripe. Prices are in USD; subscriptions bill monthly. When you check out, Stripe collects and processes your payment information under its own privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy. We receive confirmation of your purchase or subscription (such as your email and order details) so we can deliver your download, apply your plan, and provide support.

4. Sharing of information

We share personal information only as needed to run the site:

  • Stripe — payment processing (see above).
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and data storage, including account, saved-framework, and subscriber data.
  • Email — subscriber addresses are stored in the site's own database. We do not currently use a third-party email provider for subscriber mail.
  • Affiliate networks — receive referral/click data when you use an affiliate link, under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not share your information with third parties for their marketing.

5. Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies and local storage for site functionality, such as remembering your theme preference, keeping you signed in, and storing frameworks you save on your own device when you are not signed in. Affiliate links on the site may also set cookies from the relevant affiliate network to track referrals (see "Affiliate link interactions" above).

We also use analytics tools that set cookies. Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager collect usage statistics — such as pages visited, device and browser type, and how visitors find the site — so we can understand what people use and improve it. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and session-replay analytics that show how visitors interact with our pages. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, but we will receive less usage information.

6. Data retention

We keep contact and subscriber data until you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe. Account data — saved frameworks, favorites, and member pages — is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete it. Purchase and subscription records are kept as needed for accounting, tax, and legal obligations.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@ai-framework.io. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

8. Children's privacy

ai-framework.io is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
