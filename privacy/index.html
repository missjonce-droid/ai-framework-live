Your Privacy.

This notice explains the information AI Framework may receive when you visit the site, submit a form, click an external link, or purchase a product. It should be reviewed against the analytics, advertising, email, hosting, and payment services actually enabled before major marketing begins.

Information you provide

We may receive information you enter in a contact, newsletter, submission, or reporting form, such as your name, email address, product information, URLs, and message. Do not submit passwords, government identifiers, financial account numbers, medical records, confidential client data, explicit images, or unlawful material.

Information collected automatically

The site may use hosting logs, cookies, analytics tags, and similar technologies to receive device, browser, approximate location, referring page, pages viewed, and interaction information. This can help us maintain security, understand which pages are useful, and measure marketing.

Personalization tool

The Context Capsule builder is designed to run in your browser. AI Framework does not intentionally submit its fields through a form. Information you later paste into a third-party AI platform is governed by that platform's terms and privacy practices.

Payments and external services

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. AI Framework does not receive your full card number. External AI tools, affiliate destinations, embedded services, and social platforms have their own privacy practices. Review them before creating an account or uploading data.

How information may be used

  • to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the site;
  • to respond to messages, submissions, corrections, or safety reports;
  • to deliver requested products, newsletters, or service information;
  • to measure site usage, affiliate activity, and marketing performance;
  • to comply with law, protect rights, and prevent fraud or abuse.

Retention and choices

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and security. You may request access, correction, or deletion by using the site contact route; some records may need to be retained where law requires.

Children

AI Framework is not directed to children under 13. The Advanced section is restricted to adults age 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Advanced section.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, contact AI Framework. Include “Privacy Request” in the subject and describe the request without sending sensitive documentation unless a secure method is arranged.
