Content & Listing Policy.

AI Framework is an independent editorial directory. A listing is not an endorsement, certification, guarantee of safety, or legal determination. Products can change after review. Users must verify current features, terms, legality, privacy practices, age requirements, and pricing with the provider.

General listing standards

We aim to list tools that have a clear purpose, a functioning official destination, sufficient information for a reader to understand the product, and a legitimate use case. Listings may be corrected, relabeled, restricted, noindexed, suspended, or removed at our discretion.

We may reject or remove tools associated with fraud, deceptive claims, malware, unlawful access, identity abuse, harassment, exploitation, non-consensual content, privacy violations, intellectual-property abuse, or material risks that cannot be responsibly described.

Advanced and age-restricted listings

Adults only. The Advanced section is intended for people age 18 or older. An age confirmation is a content boundary, not a guarantee that every linked product is lawful or appropriate in every location.

Advanced listings must have a plausible lawful use and enough public information to evaluate their purpose. They are kept separate from the general directory and may be excluded from search indexing and advertising inventory.

AI Framework does not permit listings or promotional copy that encourage:

  • sexual content involving or depicting minors, or attempts to age-transform minors into sexual content;
  • non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual deepfakes of identifiable people, “nudify” uses, revenge pornography, or threats to create or publish such material;
  • sexual exploitation, trafficking, coercion, or paid sexual services;
  • harassment, stalking, doxxing, blackmail, impersonation, fraud, or identity theft;
  • malware, credential theft, unauthorized access, evasion of lawful safeguards, or instructions intended to harm systems or people;
  • content or services that are illegal where offered, promoted, or used.

No listing gives a user permission to upload another person's image, voice, identity, private information, copyrighted work, or confidential data. Consent, authorization, and applicable law still govern.

Affiliate and sponsored relationships

Some links may earn AI Framework a commission. Affiliate relationships do not purchase a favorable ranking or guarantee inclusion. Sponsored placement must be labeled near the placement. See the Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure.

Corrections, reports, and removals

We provide a reporting route for inaccurate, unsafe, unlawful, deceptive, expired, or rights-infringing listings. A report should identify the exact URL, the concern, and enough information to evaluate it. Do not upload unlawful or explicit evidence; provide a safe description and a source or case reference when available.

Urgent concerns involving imminent danger should be reported to the appropriate emergency or law-enforcement authority. AI Framework is not an emergency service and cannot investigate crimes.

Review and appeals

We may temporarily restrict a listing while reviewing a credible report. Providers may request a correction and supply verifiable information. We retain editorial control over descriptions, labels, warnings, ranking, noindex decisions, and removal.
