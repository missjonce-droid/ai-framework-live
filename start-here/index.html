YOUR FIRST FIVE MINUTES

You don’t need jargon.
You need one small win.

An AI assistant is a tool you can ask to help with writing, ideas, explanations, and organizing information. Describe a small task, review what it gives you, and ask for a change. That’s a useful place to begin.

01

Choose one assistant.

Explore ChatGPT or Claude in the directory. Follow its official website link, check its current free options, and open a chat. You only need one to try this guide.

02

Try something you already do.

Copy one example below and replace the bracketed text with your own. Use a fictional example or remove private details while you’re learning.

Make an email clearer

Rewrite this email so it is clear, friendly, and concise. Keep my meaning and do not invent facts. Give me one version:

[Paste a non-sensitive draft here]

Make a task manageable

Help me break this task into five small steps. Start with the easiest action I can take in ten minutes. Ask one question if something important is missing:

[Describe the task here]

Understand a new idea

Explain [topic] to a beginner using plain language and one everyday example. Define unfamiliar terms. Tell me what I should verify with an original source.

03

Read it. Check it. Refine it.

The first answer is a draft. Check names, numbers, dates, and claims against the original information. Then try a follow-up:

“Make it shorter.”   “Use simpler language.”   “Give me another example.”   “What assumptions did you make?”

For health, legal, financial, or other consequential decisions, use qualified help and authoritative sources. Keep sensitive information out of a new tool until you understand how it handles your data.
