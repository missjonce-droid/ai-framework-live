A 15-Minute Personalization System

Make AI work like you.

Personalization is not a magic prompt. It is a small operating manual that tells an AI your context, voice, boundaries, decision rules, and definition of good work—without handing over your private life.

Build your Context Capsule Learn the five layers

The Context Capsule

5 personalization layers
  • Context and current goals
  • Voice and working preferences
  • Boundaries and permissions
  • Evidence and quality rules

The Method

Personal, not private.

Give the AI stable working context, not secrets. Never include passwords, financial account numbers, Social Security numbers, private medical records, confidential client data, or anything you would not want stored by the platform.

Layer 01

Identity & context

Your role, experience level, current projects, audience, and the real-world constraints surrounding the work.

Layer 02

Goals & priorities

What you are trying to achieve now, what matters most, what “done” means, and how tradeoffs should be handled.

Layer 03

Voice & format

How you communicate, words you avoid, desired level of detail, and whether you prefer prose, checklists, tables, drafts, or decisions.

Layer 04

Boundaries & permissions

What the AI must never assume, which actions require approval, sensitive topics, and where a qualified professional is needed.

Layer 05

Quality & memory hygiene

How to label uncertainty, verify changing facts, challenge weak reasoning, and periodically remove instructions that are outdated or no longer useful.

Reality Check

Personalization is not truth

A personalized answer can still be wrong. For legal, medical, financial, safety, and current-information questions, verify with authoritative sources and a qualified human.

Make It Better
Test, compare, refine.

      
Run one real task before and after adding the capsule. Keep the instructions that measurably improve the result. Delete preferences that make the AI overly agreeable, repetitive, or unable to challenge you.

    

    

      
Test 01
Consistency
Does the answer follow your format and voice across three different tasks without constant reminders?

      
Test 02
Judgment
Does it recognize tradeoffs, flag missing information, and disagree when your plan has a serious weakness?

      
Test 03
Usefulness
Can you use the deliverable immediately, or does it still produce polished-sounding filler?
