About AI Framework

AI Framework is an independent digital publication covering artificial intelligence through curated news, field guides, tool directories, and educational resources. We highlight trustworthy reporting from leading technology publishers and provide clear, accessible context for readers navigating the rapidly evolving AI landscape.

Editorial Collaboration

AI Framework collaborates with journalists, editors, and media organizations covering artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, and digital policy.

  • Cross-promotion with established technology publications
  • Inclusion in AI-related reporting
  • Opportunities to highlight responsible AI journalism
  • Participation in stories involving AI tools, ethics, or public impact

We feature and link to reporting from outlets such as MIT Technology Review, TechCrunch, The Verge, and Ars Technica. We do not republish full articles; external journalism is credited and linked to its original source.

Ethics & Standards

AI Framework follows clear editorial guidelines:

  • We do not accept incentives that influence coverage
  • AI-assisted content is human-reviewed
  • We correct errors transparently
  • Sponsored content is clearly labeled
  • We respect and support original journalism
  • We do not sell user data or use deceptive design

Questions about a story, correction, sponsorship disclosure, or sourcing decision can be sent to press@ai-framework.io.
