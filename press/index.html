About AI Framework

AI Framework is an independent resource for people evaluating and applying AI. The site combines a curated tool directory with practical guides, playbooks, and selected industry coverage.

Editorial collaboration

We welcome inquiries from journalists, researchers, editors, and organizations working on AI-related reporting or education.

External reporting featured by AI Framework is credited and linked to its original source. We do not republish full articles.

Ethics & Standards

AI Framework follows clear editorial guidelines:

  • We do not accept incentives that influence coverage
  • AI-assisted content is human-reviewed
  • We correct errors transparently
  • Sponsored content is clearly labeled
  • We respect and support original journalism
  • We do not sell user data or use deceptive design

Questions about a story, correction, sponsorship disclosure, or sourcing decision can be sent to press@ai-framework.io.

Partnership opportunities
