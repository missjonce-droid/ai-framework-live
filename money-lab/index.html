Start here: #1 The AI Business Accuracy Detective

TOP PICK FOR AI FRAMEWORK

Find businesses with outdated hours, menus, prices, or online information. Sell a verified correction and maintenance service. Test price: $99–$399 per audit.

  1. Find 10 businesses and check their public listings against official sources.
  2. Build a before/after report showing exactly what's wrong, with evidence.
  3. Contact the owners — lead with the proof, not a pitch.
  4. Offer a free three-error sample. Charge for fixing and monitoring.

The trick: charge for fixing and monitoring — never claim control over what ChatGPT or any AI assistant answers. Nobody outside those companies controls their answers. The honest product is corrected sources plus monthly re-checks.
